# Adaptable design standard

Define these private values before directing a room:

- whole-home style, palette, materials, and finish rules;
- room function, circulation, fixed construction, and measured clearances;
- existing furniture, approved purchases, rejected options, and budget;
- original-photo views, measured drawings, and reusable render bases.

For each candidate, assess fit, function, visual weight, material and color
coherence, maintenance, delivery risk, price, and reversibility. Call out the
deciding tradeoff. Use current product sources for dimensions, availability,
and price.

Generated images answer composition questions only. They do not verify scale,
clearance, construction, color accuracy, or product availability.
